                Guide
                
          
        
                
        
                
          
                  
            
                1. 
              
                  Media Manager
                  
              
                  Click Media Manager. Drop a video on the drop area, or click it to choose a file. The app fits it to 1920×1080, compresses it for smooth playback, and saves it into the Media Library. Wait until the row finishes. To skip the conversion, click Bypass Transcode & Add Directly. Transcoding runs in the desktop app.
                  
            
                  2. 
            
                2. 
              
                  Project: audio and media
                  
              
                  Open File. New Project starts clean. Load Project opens a saved show. Save and Save New write the desk, the scenes, and the left-column settings into a project file.
                  
              
                  Open the Audio fold. Click Live Hardware and pick an input, or click Local File and drop an .mp3, .wav, or .aiff. Press Play. Auto-Gain keeps the level steady. Master Gain and Mute set how hard the sound hits the picture.
                  
              
                  Open Media Library. Click + Add media files, or use the clips Media Manager just made. On a card, click A, B, or C to put that clip on a layer.
                  
            
                  3. 
            
                3. 
              
                  Layers
                  
              
                  Under the preview are layers A, B, and C. Click Select (or press Q, W, or E). The mix menu sets how this layer sits on the one below: Normal, Screen, Multiply, and the rest. Raise the opacity fader to bring it in. Mute and Solo cut or isolate a layer. Sync locks the video to the master Play, Pause, and Stop.
                  
            
                  4. 
            
                4. 
              
                  Shaders
                  
              
                  With a layer selected, pick a look in the Inspector, or press 1–9 for the first nine. Each look is a shader, from Glitch and Y2K / 2000s through Black Metallic Y2K. On the 3D looks, drag the preview to orbit and scroll to zoom.
                  
            
                  5. 
            
                5. 
              
                  Inspector
                  
              
                  The Inspector is the column on the right. Work one layer at a time. The Media menu assigns the video: Test pattern, a camera, an NDI / Spout source, or a library clip. Mirror flips it. The groups under that hold the sliders for the current look. Hover a slider to read what it changes. Mute bypasses a group. 🎲 Shuffle randomizes that group. Double-click a slider to reset it. Then Select the next layer and repeat.
                  
            
                  6. 
            
                6. 
              
                  Audio reactivity
                  
              
                  Sound can move the picture in more than one place at once. On a layer strip, pick Sub-Bass, Mid-Energy, High-Hats, LFO 1, or LFO 2, then set Depth and Gate. In the Inspector, each slider has its own audio row. Routes include Punch, BPM Pulse, Song Energy, Drop Pulse, and Beat / Onset. Depth is how far the slider moves. Gate ignores quiet sound. Click A on a slider to add an LFO on top. The Audio fold meters show KICK, SNARE, NRG, BRK, and DROP as the sound comes in.
                  
            
                  7. 
            
                7. 
              
                  Diagnostics
                  
              
                  Click Diagnostics in the top bar. Helper / Diagnostics keeps updating while you work. What's happening lists what is driving each control. What's messy flags clipping and stacked routes. Performance and Keep it readable flag load and hard-to-read setups. Click a warning to jump to that slider.
                  
            
                  8. 
            
                8. 
              
                  BPM
                  
              
                  The clock sits at the top of Composition. It starts at 120.0. Click Auto: Read Live to take the tempo from the audio. Tap/Phase (T) sets a tempo, or one tap on the beat lines the phase up. x2 and /2 double or halve it. Nudge + and Nudge - move it by 0.1. The beat light flashes on each beat. Shaders, the strobe, and the timeline read this clock.
                  
            
                  9. 
            
                9. 
              
                  Composition
                  
              
                  Composition is the last pass, after the three layers are mixed. Set Master Speed (1.00x is normal, 0 freezes motion) and Output Brightness. Then the master groups: Color & Texture, Distortion & Glitch, and Motion & Timing. Strobe Source can be Manual, Beat Pulse, or Drop Pulse. Lock a group if Shuffle should leave it alone.
                  
            
                  10. 
            
                10. 
              
                  Output and recording
                  
              
                  Open the Output fold. Pick the Preview shape. Click Master Output and choose a display. Pick an NDI / Syphon / SPOUT size and click Send when another app should receive the picture. Click Advanced Output to drag the corner pins onto a projector.
                  
              
                  Open Recordings. Set Scale to Fill or Fit, and Render scale to 0.5x, 0.75x, or 1x. Pick the format and leave Include audio on if the file should have sound. In the top bar, pick 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, or Native, then click ● REC. Click ■ STOP to finish.
